# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of **`git-on-cloudflare`** and the **[Vib Tools](https://vibtools.io/)** community pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, practical, and healthy technical community aligned with our philosophy: **Build Useful Tools. Share Them Openly.**

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- Demonstrating empathy, kindness, and respect toward other developers and users.
- Focusing on practical, technical, evidence-based feedback and constructive code reviews.
- Respecting differing viewpoints, experiences, and operational workflows.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback on issues and pull requests.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Respecting repository architecture, documentation, security disclosure rules, and licensing boundaries.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, private repository details, credentials, or internal data, without their explicit permission.
- Intentionally disclosing unpatched security vulnerabilities in public issues rather than following [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md).
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional technical setting.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers at **Vib Tools** (`@vibtoolshq`) are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and official community channels) and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project or its community in public spaces.

---

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at **Vib Tools**:

- **General Company Contact:** [hello@vibtools.io](mailto:hello@vibtools.io)
